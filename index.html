Our Story

More than a meal —
a tradition.

Tacos Safary was born from the kitchen of a family that has been perfecting Mexican flavors for generations. Every taco, every salsa, every tortilla is made with the same love and craft that started it all.

Located in the vibrant heart of Huntington Park, we're proud to serve our community with honest, bold food that feels like home — no matter where you're from.

IF you want to go back in time, enjoy a warm motherly meal, you've found it.
